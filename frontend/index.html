Good to know

How are partial days charged?

Each started 24-hour period counts as one rental day.

What is the security deposit?

A refundable amount held during your rental and returned after the car comes back undamaged. It is never counted as revenue.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Pending and confirmed bookings can be cancelled from your dashboard before pickup.
